# King of the Screen. Sanctions and Financial Features Policy

**Effective date:** 5 September 2026

**Status:** Pre-launch policy. Financial features remain off.

## 1. No financial features today

The site does not accept SOL, transmit value for users, exchange crypto assets, hold user balances, custody private keys, process withdrawals, operate a token sale, distribute KOTS, or conduct KOTS market activity.

## 2. Future paid launch gate

No paid feature opens until all items below are complete:

1. The operator publishes the final product terms, refund terms, privacy notice, contact channel, and countries served.
2. The payment flow sends value only for the operator's own disclosed product. The service does not accept value for later forwarding, exchange, or custody for a user.
3. The operator adopts an address-screening and sanctions-escalation process proportionate to the countries served and actual transaction flow.
4. The operator defines blocked and rejected transaction handling, record retention, and lawful reporting procedures.
5. A qualified lawyer reviews the operator, customer geography, flow of funds, marketing, final terms, NFT design, and state-law exposure.

## 3. KOTS separation

KOTS is outside the first season. The site will not create, advertise, distribute, reward, buy back, provide liquidity for, or discuss future price of KOTS in connection with a throne purchase. The previous Pump.fun mint is not part of the pre-launch product.

## 4. No circumvention

The project will not use geographic, technical, or contractual language to conceal a prohibited activity or to evade applicable sanctions, consumer, securities, tax, or money-transmission requirements.

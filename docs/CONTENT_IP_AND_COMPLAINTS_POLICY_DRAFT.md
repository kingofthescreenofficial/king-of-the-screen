# King of the Screen. Content, IP, and Complaints Policy

**Effective date:** 5 September 2026

**Status:** Pre-launch policy. The site accepts no public content today.

## 1. Current status

No visitor can submit a message, image, link, wallet address, or payment through the public site. The project will not publish public user content until the paid product, moderation workflow, contact channel, and final terms are active.

## 2. Rules for any future submission

If submissions open, a participant must submit only content they have the right to use. The participant must not submit:

1. Child sexual abuse material, non-consensual intimate imagery, or other unlawful sexual content.
2. Threats, targeted harassment, terrorism support, hate-based violence, or instructions for violent wrongdoing.
3. Phishing, wallet drainers, malware, fraud, impersonation, fake token sales, or deceptive financial claims.
4. Illegal goods or services, including controlled substances and stolen goods.
5. Material that violates copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, contract, or other third-party rights.
6. Content that links to destinations prohibited by these rules.

## 3. Moderation and removal

The project can reject, remove, disable, or preserve content when it reasonably believes the content violates these rules, creates a security risk, violates law, or is the subject of a credible rights complaint. Removal does not create a financial claim, a right to an NFT, a right to KOTS, or a right to future placement.

The project will use human review and technical screening where appropriate. Those measures reduce risk. They do not guarantee that all harmful or infringing content is detected.

## 4. Copyright and trademark complaints

The pre-launch site has no public upload flow and no appointed DMCA agent. A paid launch that hosts public material requires a designated-agent decision, a published notice address, a compliant notice process, and a counter-notice process before content acceptance begins.

## 5. No financial promotion

Public content and project copy must not state or imply that a throne, NFT, KOTS, or any other digital asset will create profit, price growth, liquidity, a buyback, a return, or a share of project funds.
